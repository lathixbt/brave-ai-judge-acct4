> The snow came up to my knees on the path.
> I stopped at the gate and the cold bit my cheeks.
> The field was white and still.
> Smoke rose from the chimney in one slow line.
> My boots held on the packed snow.
> The gate swung open on its hinges, easy as ever.
> I walked through.

A short text for make.fun: a cold path, a gate that opens easy as ever, and one small step through it. Fear, patience, then the gate itself doing the holding — and a single brave step at the end. The entry program (`main.py`, Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes the text to `entry.txt`.
